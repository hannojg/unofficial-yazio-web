Nutrition Desk · YAZIO Connect

1. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions).
2. Turn on Developer mode.
3. Click Load unpacked and select this folder.
4. Open https://nutrition-desk-hanno.equalcandy26.chatgpt.site in the same browser.
5. Click Connect YAZIO and finish your usual Apple sign-in in the new YAZIO tab.

The helper only hands off a session for the YAZIO tab opened by Connect, for up to 10 minutes. It sends that session only to your private Nutrition Desk tab. It does not store passwords or tokens, inspect other websites, or send analytics. Pending pairing information is kept in memory by Chrome and cleared when either tab closes or the connection succeeds. The encrypted session stays in an HttpOnly cookie in your desk browser. Disconnect from Nutrition Desk to remove it.

If YAZIO already shows your account, log out on the YAZIO page and sign in again so a new session is issued. Keep the desk tab open while signing in.

This is an independent personal tool, unaffiliated with YAZIO. Its private API can change.
